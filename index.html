Our story

Learned on the shoreline, ground by hand

Along the Konkan coast, spice is part of the tide: chillies dry on rooftops, kokum is split in the monsoon, and every household keeps its own masala. We source from the farms that grow them and mill in small lots, so nothing sits on a shelf for a year.
